Account and Data Deletion Request

B2B Souq app · Developer/entity: B2B Souq

Option 1: Inside the app (immediate)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you can't access the app, email faisal.saleh.oa@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Souq account" from the email address registered on your account, and include your username. We will verify and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile and photo.
  • Your offers and requests, with their images and videos.
  • Your conversations and their attachments.

Notes

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Temporary backups may remain with our hosting provider for a limited time before they are permanently removed.

See the Privacy Policy for more details.
